I love chinchillas, they're the coolest things I've ever seen...well not really, but they're really cute. a small subspecies of rodents packed with dense, fluffy fur fill me with loads of joy just staring at them. Did you know that because of their dense fur they take dust baths over ones with water? This has to do with because of their fur holding moisture to a degree that may prove difficult to dry, leading to mold and mildew growth if left unchecked. I end all of this to say that chinchillas are really cool and they have a soft spot in my heart.

Habitat and Diet: Chinchillas most commonly can be found in the Andes Mountains within South America. at elevations of 3,000-5,000 meters. These little critters spelunk their ways through these rocky and sand covered peaks with little vegetation in sight. Despite that, they find strength in numbers, moving in herds while taking refuge in hillsides or hollow-like rock crevices. For their diet, they take comfort in eating on shrubbery and other plant based things like lichen, moss, leaves, stems, and shrubs. These things being relatively rich in fiber for them and aids in their digestion process while also wearing down their constantly growing teeth.
