I love chinchillas, they're the coolest things I've ever seen...well not really, but they're really cute. a small subspecies of rodents packed with dense, fluffy fur fill me with loads of joy just staring at them. Did you know that because of their dense fur they take dust baths over ones with water? This has to do with because of their fur holding moisture to a degree that may prove difficult to dry, leading to mold and mildew growth if left unchecked. I end all of this to say that chinchillas are really cool and they have a soft spot in my heart.
